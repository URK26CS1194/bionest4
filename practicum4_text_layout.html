Why BioNest?

Packaging that does not outlast its purpose

Conventional plastic packaging can stay in the environment for hundreds of years. BioNest mailers, trays and food boxes break down in a compost environment in a matter of months.

Every product is made from renewable materials, printed with plant-based inks, and designed to be strong enough for shipping and food contact.
